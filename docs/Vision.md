# KABA Platform Vision

## Purpose

The KABA Platform will become the digital operating system for KABA’s commerce business.

It will connect the online store, product management, inventory, suppliers, warehouse operations, customer service, reporting, mobile applications, and AI-assisted workflows through one secure and modular platform.

## Problem being solved

KABA currently relies on separate systems, manual processes, and repeated data entry to manage products and commerce operations.

The platform is intended to reduce duplication, improve consistency, accelerate product creation, and provide one controlled integration layer between KABA and external systems.

## Product philosophy

- Automation first
- AI assisted
- Human approved
- API first
- Secure by design
- Modular and extensible
- Auditable
- Practical before complex

## Initial focus

The first capability is a secure connector between KABA and PrestaShop.

It will support product, category, image, stock, and related catalog-management operations.

## Long-term direction

The platform may later support:

- Website and mobile application backends
- Inventory and warehouse management
- Supplier and procurement workflows
- Landed-cost calculations
- Order management
- Customer-service automation
- WhatsApp commerce
- Reporting and analytics
- Marketplace integrations
- AI-assisted product and operational workflows

## Scope boundaries

The platform should not replace mature systems unnecessarily.

Where a suitable external system already exists, the KABA Platform should integrate with it rather than rebuild it without a clear business reason.

## Success criteria

The platform is successful when KABA can manage core commerce operations through consistent, secure, and increasingly automated workflows without depending on repeated manual entry across separate systems.
