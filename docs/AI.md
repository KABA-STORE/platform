# AI Services

## Purpose

The AI layer will assist with product creation and product-content management.

## Planned capabilities

- Generate product titles
- Generate short and long descriptions
- Generate SEO titles and meta descriptions
- Extract and normalize technical specifications
- Recommend categories
- Detect missing product data
- Assist with image preparation
- Identify possible duplicate products

## Safety model

AI-generated content must be validated before publication.

Products created through AI workflows should be inactive by default until reviewed and approved.
